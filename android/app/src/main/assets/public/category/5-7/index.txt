Students then progress to learning print awareness skills such as to begin reading at the top of a page, and read to the bottom.  Knowing to read words from left to right is print awareness too. 

Kids Academy has a series of videos that teach these basic skills. Beginning with the most basic of concepts, the first video teaches children that every word that is heard can be written down.
