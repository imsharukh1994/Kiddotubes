Disciplinary Core Ideas:

PS1.A: Structure and Properties of Matter - Different kinds of matter exist and many of them can be either solid or liquid, depending on temperature (2-PS1-1). A great variety of objects can be built up from a small set of pieces (2-PS1-3).

PS1.B: Chemical Reactions - Heating or cooling a substance may cause changes that can be observed. Sometimes these changes are reversible, and sometimes they are not (2-PS1-4).

Performance Expectations:

2-PS1-1. Plan and conduct an investigation to describe and classify different kinds of materials by their observable properties.
 
2-PS1-3. Make observations to construct an evidence-based account of how an object made of a small set of pieces can be disassembled and made into a new object.
 
2-PS1-4. Construct an argument with evidence that some changes caused by heating or cooling can be reversed and some cannot.

✈️ Why Can’t Airplanes Go to Space?
Airplanes need air to work. Their engines suck in air and mix it with fuel to make power.
But in space…
😲 There is no air at all!
So airplane engines would stop working.
Rockets are different!
They carry their own air and fuel, so they can fly on Earth and in space!
________________________________________

⚖️ The Simple Rule That Makes Rockets Fly
Here’s the magic rule of motion:
👉 If something pushes backward, it moves forward!
When a rocket fires, hot gas shoots out the bottom very fast.
That gas pushes down — and the rocket goes UP! 🚀⬆️
Just like a balloon:
Air goes one way → balloon goes the other way!
This rule is called Newton’s Third Law, but you can just remember:
Push back to go forward!
________________________________________

🧩 What’s Inside a Rocket?
Rockets look simple, but inside they have four important parts:
🧱 Body – Holds everything together
🎒 Payload – What the rocket carries (satellites, robots, or people!)
🧠 Computer Brain – Keeps the rocket going the right way
🔥 Engine & Fuel – Makes the powerful fire and gas
Most of the rocket is filled with fuel because it takes LOTS of power to go to space!
________________________________________

🔥 Two Types of Rocket Engines
There are two main kinds of rockets:
🎆 Solid Rockets
• Like giant fireworks
• Burn all at once
• Very simple
• Can’t be turned off
💧 Liquid Rockets
• Fuel and air stored separately
• Can be turned on and off
• Very powerful
• Used for astronauts and satellites
Big rockets often use both types together!

________________________________________
🎯 How Do Rockets Fly Straight?
A rocket must not spin or fall over while flying.
Two secret balance points help:
⚖️ Balance Point — where weight is centered
💨 Air Push Point — where air pushes the most
For safe flying, the heavy part must be in front and the air push must be in the back.
That’s why rockets have fins at the bottom — like arrows!
________________________________________

🪜 Why Do Rockets Drop Pieces While Flying?
Have you seen rockets drop big parts after takeoff? 😮
That’s called staging!
Once one fuel tank is empty, the rocket drops it to become lighter.
Then the next engine fires and goes even faster!
Less weight = more speed! ⚡🚀
It’s like throwing away an empty backpack while climbing a mountain.
________________________________________

🌟 Why Rockets Are Awesome
Rockets help us:
🛰 Send satellites
🌍 Study Earth
🌕 Visit the Moon
🔴 Explore Mars
🧑‍🚀 Send astronauts to space
All using gas pushing backward!
